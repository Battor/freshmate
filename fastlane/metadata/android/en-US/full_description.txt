FreshMate is a simple, offline-first food freshness manager. Track what is in your fridge, see at a glance what expires soon, and stop throwing away forgotten food.

<b>Features</b>

• Expiry buckets — items are grouped by remaining shelf life (expired / expiring soon / still fresh), with a clear visual countdown on every card
• Quick manual entry — add an item with name, category, production date and shelf life in seconds
• Flexible shelf life — enter days, months or years; common presets one tap away
• Three-pane editing — review what you just added, keep editing, or browse existing items with snap-scroll sections
• History & restore — removed items are kept in history and can be restored with one tap
• Expiry reminders — scheduled notifications warn you before food goes bad
• Onboarding guide — a step-by-step walkthrough of the main screen on first launch
• Themes & languages — dark and light theme, per-app language (Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English)

<b>Privacy</b>

FreshMate works fully offline. All data stays on your device — no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The only network access is an optional manual update check against the developer's website.
